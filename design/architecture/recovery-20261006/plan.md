## Dependency order
S0 is executed risk-first and precedes any production repair. S1–S6 have disjoint writers and no forward dependency; S2 retains its current public action seam, S3 retains initialize/repository signatures. S7a reads the additive SC1 visitLimitReached Boolean defined in contracts.md and otherwise existing state. S7b consumes F's fixed NavigationGpsEvent/attempt protocol and existing gpsLost state; the primary owns its three added files and provider wiring after F settled. G/H/I are disjoint follow-through leases added after confirmed authority/invalidation, stale Retry and UX-shelf gaps. H protects bytes outside G's engine lock, and I never claims runtime evidence. All candidate changes are integrated before S8. S9 baseline acquisition can proceed early without changing Pixel data; deployment needs S8 and same-signer qualification. S10 closes only after the evidenced outcomes; an unlock gate is not completion. New required write paths trigger a plan/lease amendment before mutation.
