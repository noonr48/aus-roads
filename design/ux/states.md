## Main-map lifecycle views

- **First run:** loading seam → bundled Adelaide map → explicit demo-coverage banner → top search → one-time **Hold anywhere on the map to drop a pin** teaching chip; My location remains optional.
- **Habituated:** the hold hint disappears after use; map, search, History, location, saved markers, and bottom navigation remain dense and direct.
- **Returning:** the last destination/tab state is restored where Navigation permits; saved pins/settings persist; the map reopens the current installed pack identity and reacquires only foreground location.
- **Recovery after error:** Map, Search, Nearby, routing, and active navigation keep direct Retry/Manage/**End navigation** exits; location/recalculation failures preserve prior guidance; history/pin initial reads distinguish loading, true empty, and recoverable failure; category/emergency/generic traffic queries and pin mutations retry without losing usable prior context, while credential rejection retains traffic and opens Settings; settings retains the exact failed mutation behind a shared Map/Settings Retry; chooser failure preserves share text through the clipboard; generic map-pack worker copy is stable and safe. The app-shell unexpected-destination boundary remains the sole flow-dead-end gap.

## Surfaces with more than two `N/A` cells

- **Map first-use guidance:** it is intentionally an informational teaching surface, not a data box; its meaningful lives are shown/occluded/completed.
- **Selected result banner:** it is a transient disclosure control created only from an already-valid result, so empty/loading/disabled are owned by search/Nearby and map readiness.
- **Feature/result detail and Edit pin sheets:** modal surfaces are only constructed for valid backing data; upstream surfaces own empty/loading. Their persistence error gaps are still explicit.
- **Recent routes sheet:** initial observation now owns loading/read failure/Retry, while deletion separately owns confirmation, progress, retained failure, Retry, and disabled states.
- **Theme selector, labelled switches, and API-key fields:** these always-present local controls do not have meaningful empty lives; their shared write-recovery snackbar owns failure and retry progress.
- **About:** static bundled content has one success life plus Back; retaining it as a screen keeps legal/privacy material discoverable.
